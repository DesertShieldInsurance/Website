Commercial Transportation Risk Is My Focus

I lead and manage a commercial trucking insurance program serving owner-operators, small fleets, and transportation companies.

My experience is centered on commercial transportation risk, including trucking liability exposures, fleet safety, driver qualification, commercial-auto claims, cargo operations, underwriting documentation, policy servicing, agency relationships, and insured risk-management practices.
