Where to Start

Arizona Coverage Requirements Aren't One-Size-Fits-All

Some coverage — like workers' compensation — is required by Arizona law the moment you hire your first employee. Other coverage, like general liability, isn't state-mandated but is required by nearly every lease, lender, and client contract. We help you sort out what's actually required, what's smart to carry anyway, and build a program around it.
