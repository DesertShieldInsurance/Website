Desert Shield Insurance LLC ("Desert Shield Insurance," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through desertshieldinsurance.com (the "Site"), why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have — including specific protections for phone numbers and SMS/text messaging consent.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, such as when you request a quote, contact us, or sign up for communications. This may include:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Business or fleet information relevant to an insurance inquiry (e.g., vehicle types, operating authority, coverage needs)
  • Details you include in a message or inquiry
  • Whether you have opted in to receive SMS/text messages, and the date, time, and page where that opt-in occurred

We may also automatically collect limited technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) through standard website analytics, which does not include SMS opt-in data.

2. Why We Collect It

We collect this information to respond to your inquiry, prepare an insurance quote, service a policy, communicate with you about your request, and comply with applicable insurance and legal recordkeeping requirements.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to quote requests and general inquiries
  • Provide insurance-related services, including underwriting support, policy servicing, and claims advocacy
  • Send follow-up communications you have agreed to receive, including SMS/text messages if you have opted in
  • Maintain records required for insurance licensing, compliance, and carrier documentation
  • Improve our website and services

4. SMS / Text Messaging Program

If you provide a mobile phone number and separately check the SMS opt-in checkbox on our Contact page, you consent to receive text messages from Desert Shield Insurance at that number, including messages related to your inquiry, insurance quotes, policy servicing, and follow-up communications. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

Providing a phone number alone is never treated as SMS consent. We only send marketing or informational text messages to numbers where the separate SMS opt-in checkbox was affirmatively checked. The checkbox is unchecked by default, is optional, and is never required to submit a form, request a quote, or do business with us.

You can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us directly at 480.789.1844 or Chris@desertshieldinsurance.com. Once you opt out, we honor that request and do not send further marketing or promotional text messages to that number, except any message legally permitted to confirm your opt-out.

For each SMS opt-in, we retain a record of the phone number provided, the date and time consent was given, the page/form where consent occurred, the exact consent language displayed at that time, and confirmation that the checkbox was affirmatively checked. This record is maintained to document consent and to support carrier or regulatory verification (including A2P 10DLC registration) if requested.

5. How Information May Be Disclosed

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business — for example, insurance carriers we submit applications to, agency management or CRM software providers, email or SMS delivery platforms, and website hosting providers. These service providers are only permitted to use your information to perform services on our behalf and are not authorized to use it for their own independent marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or regulatory request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Desert Shield Insurance, our customers, or others.

6. SMS Data Is Never Sold or Shared for Marketing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent collected through this website are not sold, rented, licensed, or shared with any third party or affiliate for their own marketing or promotional purposes — under any circumstance. This applies regardless of any other provision of this Privacy Policy. SMS-related data is used solely by Desert Shield Insurance to communicate with you about your own inquiry, quote, or policy.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect the personal information we collect. No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices & How to Contact Us

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, and you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us directly. To exercise any of these choices, or if you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact:

9. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. This policy does not, and will not, introduce any provision permitting the sale or third-party marketing use of SMS opt-in data described in Section 6 above.
