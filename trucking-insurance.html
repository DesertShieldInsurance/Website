Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

I just got my authority — can you write coverage for a new operation?

Yes. New authority is one of our most common quotes. We work with carriers that write new operations and can walk you through the filings (BMC-91/91X, UCR) that come with it.

Do I need every one of these coverage lines?

Not necessarily — it depends on your operation, contracts, and what your dispatcher or broker requires. We'll walk through your specific setup and only recommend what actually fits.

Can you write coverage across multiple states?

Yes — coverage is written across multiple states through multiple A-rated carriers, which also gives us room to shop your renewal for a better rate over time.

How fast can I get a certificate of insurance?

Most COI requests are turned around same-day once your policy is bound, so you're not stuck waiting on a load or a new contract.
